About

I'm a software engineer and co-founder of BloomX Analytica, where we build trusted, privacy-first AI for businesses. We focus on AI-integrated products that solve real industry problems, designed from day one to scale.

Before BloomX, I spent over five years building web applications and cloud systems with .NET Core, C#, React.js and Microsoft Azure.
